Our Story & Mission

Reviving The Love For Real Home-Cooked Food

TiffinTrail was born out of a simple craving: genuine, nourishing food cooked with the warmth and care of home, delivered consistently every single afternoon.

Empowering Neighborhood Home Cooks

In every neighborhood, there are talented home cooks whose culinary gifts rarely reach beyond their own dining tables. TiffinTrail provides these skilled chefs with the platform, hygiene standards, packaging guidelines, and delivery logistics needed to turn their culinary heritage into thriving home micro-enterprises.

When you subscribe to a tiffin on TiffinTrail, you aren't feeding commercial restaurant chains. You are directly supporting a culinary artisan in your local neighborhood.

Our Three Pillars

100% Homestyle Standards No restaurant heavy creams, no reuse of burnt fry oils, and zero artificial preservatives or food colorings.
Hygienic FSSAI Certified Kitchens Rigorous kitchen hygiene checks, clean potable water standards, and food-grade thermal containers.
Dependable Daily Schedule Thermal hot delivery straight to your office desk or home before lunch break arrives.
