Our Story & Mission

Reviving The Love For Real Home-Cooked Food

TiffinTrail was born out of a simple craving: genuine, nourishing food cooked with the warmth and care of home, delivered consistently every single afternoon.

Empowering Neighborhood Home Cooks

In every neighborhood, there are talented home cooks whose culinary gifts rarely reach beyond their own dining tables. TiffinTrail provides these skilled chefs with the platform, hygiene training, packaging standards, and delivery logistics needed to turn their culinary heritage into thriving home micro-enterprises.

When you subscribe to a tiffin on TiffinTrail, you aren't feeding commercial restaurant chains. You are supporting a local culinary artisan in your community.

Our Three Pillars

100% Homestyle Standards No restaurant heavy creams, no reuse of burnt fry oils, and zero artificial coloring agents.
Hygienic FSSAI Certified Kitchens Rigorous audits, water quality checks, and clean food-grade thermal containers.
Dependable Daily Schedule Thermal hot delivery straight to your office desk or home before lunch break.
